## Work achievement story

<article class="experience-feature">
  <div class="experience-feature__header">
    <div>
      <p class="profile-eyebrow">NLLN CO., LTD. · INTERN</p>
      <h2>Translating emerging technology into a client-ready business case</h2>
    </div>
    <span>Dec 2025 - Feb 2026</span>
  </div>

  <div class="experience-steps">
    <section>
      <h3>The challenge</h3>
      <p>Manufacturing clients were considering AI agents, machine-learning solutions, and factory automation, but adoption depended on more than technical capability. Each proposal had to connect new technology with a specific operational pain point and a realistic business need.</p>
    </section>
    <section>
      <h3>My contribution</h3>
      <p>I researched adoption trends, joined client site visits and working-level meetings, and helped organize observations about workflow constraints, root causes, and requirements. I then worked across engineering, consulting, and marketing perspectives to support sales strategies and integrated proposals.</p>
    </section>
    <section>
      <h3>What I learned</h3>
      <p>The strongest technology recommendation is not the most complex one; it is the one that people can understand, adopt, and connect to a real operating problem. This experience strengthened my interest in the human and organizational side of data-driven service innovation.</p>
    </section>
  </div>
</article>
