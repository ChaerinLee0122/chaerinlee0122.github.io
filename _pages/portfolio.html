Selected academic projects showing how I frame complex service and tourism problems, work with evidence, and translate analysis into practical recommendations.

Tourism Resources · ESG · Business Strategy

ESG-Driven Regeneration for Let's Run Park Seoul

Developed a master plan to transform the Korea Racing Authority's flagship site into an ESG-integrated sports theme park, with a focus on non-race-day activation and new audiences.

  • Structured uses for 340 acres of idle assets through a Fishbone Diagram, including e-sports, motion simulators, and glamping.
  • Designed a 130-person operating plan and an ESG governance framework covering retired racehorse welfare and zero-waste local F&B partnerships.
  • Mapped CAPEX priorities across ecological programs, running grounds, and immersive media centers within public-corporation revenue constraints.
Tourism Policy · Financial Feasibility · UGC

World Youth Day 2027: Global Youth Seoul Living Lab

Created a policy proposal that turns a global youth event into a longer-term platform for local living, cultural exchange, and city storytelling.

  • Combined PEST, SWOT, VRIO, and ESG analysis with a ₩115M budget and ₩150M+ estimated tourist expenditure, indicating 130%+ projected ROI.
  • Designed a multilingual UGC reporter system to engage young people from 100+ nationalities as city ambassadors.
  • Proposed a one-month "Living Like a Local" stay package for Gen Z visitors.
Marketing Analytics · Privacy · Ad Attribution

Amazon Shopper Panel and the Future of Consumer Data

Analyzed Amazon's effort to connect off-platform retail behavior with digital advertising performance in a cookieless environment.

  • Benchmarked the service against Nielsen, Circana/NPD, Google Opinion Rewards, and Ibotta.
  • Evaluated data exclusivity, account-level identity integration, and attribution capabilities.
  • Examined GDPR, CCPA, antitrust, tracking, and data-value fairness trade-offs.
AI · Smart Tourism · Regional Development

AI-Powered Emotion-Based Stayover Tourism Cluster

Proposed a smart-tourism framework for Chung-Buk Province designed to convert day trips into longer stays while preserving cultural authenticity.

  • Integrated traveler emotion and fatigue indicators, mobility patterns, AR engagement, and location-based activity data.
  • Designed healing, nighttime, and workation clusters to support multi-day itineraries.
  • Included resident-led AR experiences co-created with local elders and artisans.
Geodemographics · Hospitality Marketing

Nielsen PRIZM for Hotel Marketing

Applied lifestyle-based segmentation to hospitality marketing, moving beyond conventional demographic categories to explain consumer motivations.

  • Profiled the PRIZM Y1-34 segment for Marriott International.
  • Designed targeted upsell packages, fitness partnerships, and digital channel strategies.
  • Identified localization and travel-context limitations in adapting the U.S. model abroad.
Platform Strategy · Service Operations

Market Evolution of the Food Delivery Industry

Studied how delivery platforms compete on fee structures, subscription retention, and multidimensional service quality.

  • Used the SERVQUAL framework to assess customer experience and growth models.
  • Compared platform business models and market-survival factors.
  • Forecasted quick commerce, autonomous last-mile logistics, and IP-driven ghost kitchens.
Luxury Hospitality · Brand Strategy

Aman Group: Global Brand Strategy

Audited Aman's evolution from remote eco-sanctuaries to an expanded luxury ecosystem spanning Janu, Aman Club, and Aman Yacht.

  • Benchmarked Aman against Six Senses and COMO Hotels in wellness and ESG governance.
  • Evaluated membership and lifestyle retail as revenue-growth strategies.
  • Considered how expansion could affect exclusivity and brand prestige.
Leadership · Organizational Change

Leadership at Marriott and Chaumet

Used leadership theory to compare how executives respond to scaling, crisis, heritage, and strategic transformation.

  • Compared Bill Marriott and Arne Sorenson through behavioral theory and the universality hypothesis.
  • Analyzed the shift between relationship-driven culture and task-oriented governance across M&A, asset-light growth, and pandemic response.
  • Examined three leadership eras at Chaumet and the role of archival heritage in organizational renewal.
